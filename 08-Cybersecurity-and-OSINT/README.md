## Why this folder exists

I build tools that protect people: **[Digital Footprint Hub](https://github.com/StynerStiner/digitalfootprinthub)** (OSINT and personal-safety verification) and **[OSINT Café](https://github.com/StynerStiner/OSINTCafe-main)** (spotting romance scams and fake profiles before someone gets hurt). To defend against attackers, you have to understand how they work.
